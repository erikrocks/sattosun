About IA Hawks

Iowa football on Saturday. Every former Hawkeye in the NFL on Sunday. One page, made for your phone.

What's here

  • Saturday is the Hawkeyes' own season: record, ranking and Big Ten standing, the last and next game, season leaders, the full schedule with a box score and scoring summary for every game, and Iowa football headlines from around the web.
  • Sunday is every former Hawkeye on an NFL roster, grouped by this week's games, with live scores and each player's stat line.

Getting around

  • Switch between Saturday and Sunday with the tabs at the bottom of the screen.
  • Tap any player or game row to open it. On Sunday, "Season stats" inside a player's row opens his whole NFL season, game by game.
  • Tap the week badge at the top of the Sunday tab to look back at earlier weeks.
  • The chips under the search box filter the list: live games, finals, byes, every player, season leaders, and roster moves.
  • Dimmed players aren't expected to play. "Reserve" means a reserve list such as injured reserve. "Practice sq." means the practice squad. Red tags are that week's injury report.
  • Kickoff times are shown in your own time zone.
  • Upcoming and live games show where to watch: the TV network, its streaming service, and the stadium. Sunday afternoon NFL games on CBS and Fox differ by area, so check your local station.
Want it on your home screen? In Safari tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". In Chrome on Android, open the menu and choose "Add to Home screen". It opens like an app.

Where the numbers come from

  • Scores, box scores, schedules and rankings come from ESPN. NFL rosters and injury tags come from Sleeper, checked against ESPN's team rosters every morning.
  • While a game is on, scores and stat lines refresh about every 45 seconds. Otherwise the data refreshes every morning.
  • Season totals and leaders are added up from each game's box score, so they can differ slightly from official totals. Players who don't appear in box scores, such as most offensive linemen, won't have stat lines.
  • "College" is the last school a player attended, so a player who transferred out of Iowa may be missing and one who transferred in is included.
  • Headlines link straight to the outlets that wrote them.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no cookies. The site counts page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or identify you. Like any website, the services that deliver it see your IP address when you load a page: GitHub (hosting), Google (the fonts), ESPN (live scores, logos and photos) and Cloudflare (the page counter).
